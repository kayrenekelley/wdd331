Our Relationship with the National Park Service

Working together to preserve America's most treasured landscapes and cultural sites for future generations.

A Partnership Built on Conservation

The National Park Foundation is the official charitable partner of the National Park Service. We work hand-in-hand with the NPS to protect and enhance America's national parks for present and future generations.

Through our partnership, we provide critical funding for park projects, support educational programs, and help connect people to these extraordinary places that tell the story of America.

Key Areas of Focus

🌲

Conservation

Protecting natural resources and wildlife habitats through innovative conservation programs and sustainable practices.

🎓

Education

Connecting people to parks through educational programs, interpretive services, and immersive learning experiences.

🤝

Community

Building strong partnerships with local communities and fostering connections between people and their parks.
